Young CEOs Gather in Onitsha for Africa Leadership Summit

Under 45 CEOs Business & Leadership Summit — 2024 Edition

Published: November 16, 2024 | Event News
Written by: Chukwuemeka Emmanuel
SHARE THIS ARTICLE

ONITSHA, Anambra State — The Under 45 CEOs Business & Leadership Summit took place on November 16, 2024, bringing together young business owners, company leaders, and government officials in Onitsha, Anambra State.

The summit's theme was "Creating a Sustainable Africa Through Active Leadership," and it focused on helping young CEOs grow their businesses while building stronger networks.

What Happened at the Summit

The 2024 event offered several key activities designed to help business leaders learn and connect with each other. Top CEOs and business experts gave talks on how to lead well, run honest businesses, and stay strong during tough times. These sessions gave attendees clear ideas on how to build businesses that last in African markets.

Business training sessions taught participants practical skills like managing money, understanding their customers, and growing their companies. The workshops focused on real-world tips rather than just theory.

Cash Prizes for Best Business Ideas

One major event was the pitching competition, where young business owners presented their plans to judges and possible investors. The winner took home ₦1,000,000, while second place received ₦500,000. This gave promising businesses both money and attention. Group discussions covered important topics like finding funding, changing how leaders think, and making a real difference in African communities.

Who Spoke at the Event

Speakers came from different backgrounds. Business coaches shared tips on running companies better. Funding experts and policy advisors talked about how to get money for businesses and deal with government rules. Successful CEOs told stories about how they grew their companies in Africa.

Results from the Summit

The event created strong networking opportunities. Many startup founders met investors, mentors, and potential partners face-to-face. Several attendees said they started new partnerships and got investor attention because of the summit.

The organizers also set up mentor matches to keep helping participants even after the event ended.

Theme: Creating a Sustainable Africa Through Active Leadership
Date: November 16, 2024 (Saturday)
Venue: Onitsha, Anambra State
Prize Pool: ₦1.5 Million
Winner: ₦1,000,000
Runner-up: ₦500,000
