UDUF Africa Holds Successful March Business Meeting, Mentorship Session, and General Election 2026

Reporting by: Ezuma Smart Chinedu PhD — Founder, Ultimate Destiny Uplifters Foundation – UDUF Africa March 14, 2026
SHARE THIS REPORT

UDUF Africa marked a significant milestone on Saturday, March 14, 2026, as it successfully hosted its March Business Meeting, Mentorship Session, and General Election at Onitsha Business School, GRA, Onitsha. The event drew young leaders, entrepreneurs, professionals, members, and guests united by a shared vision of business growth and leadership development across Africa.

The Founding President of UDUF Africa, Dr. Ezuma Smart Chinedu, hosted the meeting, welcoming participants and setting the tone with a strong emphasis on mentorship, collaboration, and the power of community-driven leadership. The energy in the room was electric as members prepared not only to learn but to shape the future of the organization through a democratic election process.

Mastering the Art of Selling

A standout highlight of the day was a business presentation delivered by Christopher Uchenna, President of Earth Manager's Group and CEO of GEEPCHRIS Bridge Enterprise. Speaking on the theme "The Art of Selling, Negotiations, Deals, Profit and Market Success: How To Sell a Competitive Product or Service," Uchenna walked participants through the core professional skills every entrepreneur needs to thrive in competitive markets.

Key areas covered included the art of powerful negotiation, striking meaningful deals, crafting indisputable offers, and executing strategic advertisement. Participants were reminded that great negotiation begins with listening — not talking — and that confidence in business is built on deep knowledge of one's product or service. A particularly practical takeaway was the advice to simplify choices for customers. Rather than overwhelming potential buyers with endless options, entrepreneurs were encouraged to present solutions clearly, ideally offering just two options at a time to make decision-making easier.

On pricing, Uchenna offered a refreshing perspective: customers rarely object to price when an offer genuinely solves their problem. When budget is the concern, the smart move is to first understand the customer's financial capacity, then tailor an offer that meets both the need and the budget. The session closed with a strong message on advertisement — that buying decisions often begin emotionally, before any logical analysis. Entrepreneurs must therefore present their products in ways that immediately show customers what to see, value, and desire.

Book Review: Built to Last

The meeting also featured an engaging book review of Built to Last: Successful Habits of Visionary Companies by Jim Collins and Jerry I. Porras, facilitated by Comr. Owan Erico Itesi. Participants explored how lasting organisations build strong values and leadership systems that carry them through generations — a timely lesson for the young entrepreneurs in the room. The discussion revolved around core ideologies, preserving the core while stimulating progress, and creating mechanisms that align culture with strategic ambition. Many attendees noted how the principles of visionary companies apply directly to building sustainable personal careers and businesses.

UDUF Africa General Election 2026: A Historic Moment

Perhaps the most anticipated segment of the day was the UDUF Africa General Election 2026, conducted peacefully and transparently with 44 eligible members voting on-site. The process stood as a testament to the organisation's commitment to democratic values, accountability, and responsible leadership. The following candidates were duly elected into office:

  • President — Comr. Uchenna Christopher (elected by the Board of Directors)
  • Vice President 1 — Comr. Dr. Edwin Obioma
  • Vice President 2 — Comr. Joshua C. Iheanacho
  • Secretary General — Comr. Precious Leonard Ani
  • Assistant Secretary General — Comr. Mrs. Gloria Odinakachukwu Ezeudo
  • Director of Finance — Comr. Chira Chinyere Christiana
  • Director of Programs — Comr. Eke Chukwuemelie Miracle
  • Assistant Director of Programs — Comr. Joshua Ikechukwu
  • Director of Media — Comr. Owan Erico Itesi
  • Assistant Director of Media — Comr. Chukwuemeke Chimaobi Emmanuel
  • Director of Assets — Onuigbo Chidubem Godwin
  • Assistant Director of Assets — Comr. Ikenna Valentine Ohagwu
  • Director for Women Affairs — Comr. Mary Okoye
  • Assistant Director for Women Affairs — Comr. Grace Chinemerem Ogbu

UDUF Africa congratulates all elected officials and wishes them success as they step into their new roles. The organisation also extends its appreciation to every member who participated in making the election a credible and peaceful process. This transition marks a new chapter of member-driven leadership, ensuring that the foundation's mission continues to expand across Africa.

Looking Ahead: Momentum and Impact

Beyond the formal sessions, the day featured lively parliamentary discussions, mentorship conversations, and networking that further cemented bonds among members and guests. The UDUF Africa March 2026 Business Meeting was more than an event — it was a declaration of momentum. With a newly elected leadership in place and a community of motivated young entrepreneurs energised by practical knowledge and shared purpose, UDUF Africa is poised for its next chapter of growth and impact.

Get Inspired. Transform Africa. The foundation extends an open invitation to aspiring leaders and entrepreneurs to join the movement and be part of the upcoming April session — where new leadership will present their strategic roadmap for the year ahead.
