Report on the Successful Hosting of the UDUF Africa Active Leadership & Entrepreneurship Bootcamp 2026

Innovate, Lead and Grow: Strategies for Entrepreneurial Success

Published: February 2026 | Leadership Training
Written by: Dr Smart Chinedu Ezuma
SHARE THIS ARTICLE

Program Overview

The UDUF Africa Active Leadership & Entrepreneurship Bootcamp 2026 was successfully held on Thursday 26th and Friday 27th February 2026 at the Onitsha Business School, Onitsha, Anambra State. The two-day intensive programme achieved its primary objective of equipping participants with practical leadership, entrepreneurial, and business growth strategies.

The bootcamp attracted entrepreneurs, business owners, students, and emerging leaders from diverse sectors, all eager to learn, connect, and grow. From the opening session to the closing remarks, the atmosphere was energetic, focused, and highly engaging. Participants demonstrated enthusiasm, active participation, and a strong commitment to personal and professional development.

The theme, "Innovate, Lead and Grow: Strategies for Entrepreneurial Success", guided all sessions and discussions. Facilitators provided practical, real-world insights that addressed innovation, leadership effectiveness, financial intelligence, and sustainable business growth. Each session focused on tools and strategies participants could immediately apply to their ventures and careers.

The programme brought together a diverse group of business owners, young professionals, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs to gain knowledge on leadership, innovation, financial discipline, and enterprise growth.

Key Highlights from Facilitators

Sir Chinedu Nwaonu, President of the Onitsha Chamber of Commerce, emphasised the importance of strategic planning and disciplined execution in building sustainable businesses, urging participants to focus on systems that produce consistent results.

Prof Olusegun Oludapo Sogbesan, Director General of Onitsha Business School, shared insights on long-term strategic management and organisational leadership, stressing that successful enterprises are built on clear vision and measurable goals.

Dr Sylvanus Allegbo Izebhokae, Financial Expert and Registrar of the school, guided participants on financial discipline, business structures, and sustainability, noting that weak financial management – not poor ideas – is often the reason businesses fail.

Mrs Abayo Angela Ifeoma, Managing Director of Chillis Foods Ltd, delivered a session on Scaling Your Business: From Idea to Growth and Impact, highlighting how structured growth, market positioning, and systems create lasting value and social impact.

Amb. Dr Simeon Kelvin Chukwu, CEO of Crystal Garden Estate, presented Sales Mastery: Turning Ideas into Revenue, focusing on practical selling strategies, customer psychology, and converting opportunities into consistent income.

Dr Ezuma Smart Chinedu, Business Development Strategist and Founder of UDUF Africa, delivered a strategic session on Designing Your Business for Profit: Using the Business Model Approach. He explained that profitable businesses are intentionally designed, and provided participants with tools to structure enterprises for clarity, efficiency, and sustainable growth.

Dr Ezuma Smart Chinedu also anchored the programme by connecting all sessions with strategic business and leadership insights, creating a clear pathway for growth.

The bootcamp's facilitators brought rich professional experience and credibility to the programme, ensuring practical learning outcomes for all participants.

Remarks from the Organisers

Dr Ezuma Smart Chinedu, Founding President of UDUF Africa, highlighted that the bootcamp was not merely a training event but a platform for transformation, preparing Africa's next generation of leaders who combine competence, integrity, and service.

Comrade ThankGod Chukwuekezie Chukwuemeka, Executive Director of the Bootcamp and CEO of Rise Angel Tech Solution, described the programme as a deliberate investment in people and ideas, equipping participants with practical skills, the right mindset, and access to valuable networks. He noted that empowered youth become catalysts for economic growth and community development.

Networking & Outcomes

A major highlight was the vibrant networking environment, which encouraged new professional connections, partnership discussions, and renewed clarity about business direction and leadership journeys. Feedback from participants confirmed that the bootcamp exceeded expectations, offering practical, inspiring, and transformational learning experiences.

The successful execution of this bootcamp reflects UDUF Africa's commitment to nurturing ethical, skilled, and purpose-driven leaders and entrepreneurs across the continent. It further strengthens the organisation's reputation as a leading platform for leadership development, entrepreneurial capacity building, and community transformation.

Conclusion

The UDUF Africa Active Leadership & Entrepreneurship Bootcamp 2026 was not just an event, but a milestone experience. It successfully delivered knowledge, built confidence, fostered networks, and inspired action. The impact of this programme is expected to extend far beyond the two days as participants begin applying the lessons learnt in their businesses, careers, and communities.

Date: February 26–27, 2026
Venue: Onitsha Business School, Onitsha, Anambra State
Facilitators: 6 Expert Speakers
Participants: Entrepreneurs, Business Owners, Students, Emerging Leaders
