International Youth Day

United Nations established International Youth Day to recognise the strength, ideas and leadership of young people across the world. It is observed every year on 12 August. The day also draws attention to important matters affecting young people, including education, employment, innovation, civic responsibility and well being.

International Youth Day at UDUF Africa

At UDUF Africa (Ultimate Destiny Uplifters Foundation), this day carries deep meaning. It is not just a global observance. It is a moment to celebrate young Africans who are building businesses, leading projects, serving communities and shaping the future with courage and vision.

What We Celebrate at UDUF Africa

  • Youth Innovation and Enterprise — We celebrate young entrepreneurs who are creating solutions, generating employment and contributing to economic growth across Africa.
  • Leadership in Community Transformation — We recognise young leaders who are solving local problems, organising development initiatives and inspiring positive change in their communities.

Through keynotes, mentorship, dialogue sessions, awards, and youth-driven activities, UDUF Africa uses International Youth Day to spotlight young Africans' creativity, resilience, and capacity to lead change across society.
