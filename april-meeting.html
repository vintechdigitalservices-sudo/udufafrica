From Hustle to Success: UDUF Africa April Business Meeting Delivers Powerful Lessons on Business Growth and Leadership

Reporting by: Ezuma Smart Chinedu PhD — Founder, Ultimate Destiny Uplifters Foundation – UDUF Africa April 11, 2026
SHARE THIS REPORT

UDUF Africa held another impactful edition of its monthly Business Meeting and Mentorship Session on Saturday, April 11, 2026, at Onitsha Business School, GRA, Onitsha. The event recorded a strong turnout of members, guests, young entrepreneurs, and leaders who gathered with a shared commitment to growth, learning, and personal transformation. The atmosphere was engaged and enthusiastic throughout, with participants actively taking part in every session.

Real Stories, Real Strategies: The Theme Talk

The headline presentation of the day came from Chief Okwudilichukwu Moedu, MSc, FMC, President and CEO of Orange Express Ltd, who delivered the theme talk titled "From Hustle to Success: Real Success Stories and Strategies from a Successful Trader." His session was rich with practical knowledge, lived business experience, and strategies that participants could immediately take away and apply.

Chief Moedu opened with a powerful premise: success does not thrive in the comfort zone. He made clear that business success or failure is largely a product of mindset — what you believe you can or cannot achieve plays a defining role in your outcomes. Before pursuing success physically, he urged participants to prepare for it internally.

He spoke candidly about the role of mentorship in his own journey, crediting his father as a foundational pillar in his business development, and encouraged participants not to travel the road alone. Partnerships, he insisted, are not optional extras but vital components of sustainable growth and expansion.

On the subject of money management, his advice was direct: funds left in savings without purpose become unproductive. He challenged participants to invest wisely and channel their resources into ventures that create real value — developing business activities that are deeply aligned with the genuine needs of their customers, not just what they want to sell, but what the market truly needs.

Technology, he noted, is a partner that modern entrepreneurs cannot afford to underestimate. He pushed participants to embrace it fully as a tool for improving efficiency and scaling growth. And running through all of this was a consistent message about excellence — the discipline to always give your best, and the patience to keep improving even when results are slow to show.

Chief Moedu summarised the demands of success in terms that left no room for ambiguity:

  • A never-give-up spirit and the willingness to take calculated risks
  • Wise time management — treating time as an opportunity to be maximised
  • Discipline, patience, and consistency in all efforts
  • Visibility and proper positioning in the marketplace
  • Continuous excellence and improvement in performance

Book Review: Think and Grow Rich by Napoleon Hill

Another standout segment of the programme was the book review session on Think and Grow Rich by Napoleon Hill, facilitated by Comr. Precious Leonard Ani, CEO of Leopresh Creative Ventures. The session was described by attendees as creative, engaging, and deeply practical.

Comr. Ani walked participants through Napoleon Hill's 13 principles of success in a clear and accessible way, giving participants a framework they could begin applying to their own lives and businesses immediately. The principles covered were:

  • Desire, Faith, and Auto-Suggestion
  • Specialised Knowledge and Imagination
  • Organised Planning and Decision
  • Persistence and the Mastermind Alliance
  • Transmutation of Energy and the Subconscious Mind
  • The Brain and the Sixth Sense

The session's creative delivery ensured participants left not just with ideas, but with a genuine understanding of how each principle operates in real business and personal life scenarios.

Founder's Remarks

In his address, UDUF Africa Founding President, Ezuma Smart Chinedu, PhD, challenged participants to be intentional about both their lives and their businesses. He made a pointed observation: what you do not truly value and commit to will not succeed in your hands. He encouraged members to treat their businesses and careers with the same seriousness they bring to their personal relationships.

He further stated that UDUF Africa Business Meetings are deliberately designed to inspire members to take action, transform their lives, and become agents of positive change in their communities. He also highlighted one of the less-celebrated benefits of the monthly meetings — the access they provide to high-calibre, purpose-driven professionals. The invited theme discussants, he noted, are people of proven success, representing genuine mentorship opportunities, potential partnerships, and connections that can meaningfully accelerate members' growth.

Parliamentary Session & Interactive Discussions

The programme also featured a structured parliamentary session that gave participants room to contribute ideas, ask questions, and engage in meaningful discussion. The interactive format ensured that every attendee had the opportunity to engage directly with facilitators and experienced business leaders, reinforcing UDUF Africa's commitment to participatory and practical learning.

Networking & Mentorship

The networking and mentorship segment provided further opportunity for meaningful connections and collaborations that are expected to generate lasting value well beyond the event itself. The meeting was noted for its punctuality, strong organisation, and consistent professionalism throughout, with participants expressing high satisfaction with both the quality of the programme and the value they gained from attending.

Conclusion & Outlook

The April Business Meeting reinforced what UDUF Africa has been building month after month — a platform where visionary leaders and entrepreneurs are inspired, equipped, and connected to drive real change in their communities and across Africa. The knowledge shared, the relationships formed, and the inspiration ignited will continue to bear fruit long after the day ended.

UDUF Africa remains dedicated to continuous growth, learning, and empowerment.
