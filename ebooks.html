🌱 CURRENT BOOK OF THE MONTH • May 2026

HOW SUCCESSFUL PEOPLE GROW

John C. Maxwell

15 actionable ways to accelerate your personal growth. John Maxwell shares the mindset shifts that turn average into extraordinary.

🌿 Book Summary: How Successful People Grow

Leadership expert John C. Maxwell argues that intentional growth is the only path to your potential. He outlines 15 practical "growth keys": become a growth junkie, develop self-awareness, find your why, embrace discomfort, learn from setbacks, and more.

Core insights: The Intentional Growth Key, The Pain Key, The Ladder Key, The Rubber Band Key, The Trade-Off Key, and The Curiosity Key. Successful people never stop asking questions and stretching beyond their comfort zone.

Conclusion: You cannot lead others further than you have grown yourself. Commit to daily growth rituals to transform your career, relationships, and mindset.

🔥 Book of the Month • April 2026

THINK AND GROW RICH

Napoleon Hill

Timeless blueprint for wealth & mindset mastery. Based on Andrew Carnegie's secret, this classic reveals the philosophy of personal achievement.

📘 Definitive Summary: Think and Grow Rich

Napoleon Hill studied 500+ self-made millionaires and synthesized a 13-step formula: Burning Desire, Faith, Autosuggestion, Specialized Knowledge, Imagination, Organized Planning, Decision, Persistence, Power of the Mastermind, and the mysterious Sixth Sense. The core philosophy: whatever the mind can conceive and believe, it can achieve.

Key Takeaway: Riches begin as a state of mind. By controlling your thoughts and applying these principles, anyone can rewrite their financial destiny.

📖 Book of the Month • March 2026

BUILT TO LAST

Jim Collins & Jerry I. Porras

Visionary companies don't just survive — they endure for generations. Discover the hidden principles of institutional greatness.

🔍 Core Summary: Built to Last

Collins & Porras reveal that visionary companies build "clocks, not time-telling" — sustainable systems rooted in core ideology. Key concepts: Big Hairy Audacious Goals (BHAGs), preserve the core while stimulating progress, cult-like cultures, and the "Genius of the AND" (embracing both purpose and profit).

Takeaway: Lasting greatness comes from building institutional resilience, not relying on individual star power.

📘 Past Selection • February 2026

GOOD TO GREAT

Jim Collins

Why some companies make the leap from average to extraordinary — and why others don't.

🏆 Good to Great – Summary

Jim Collins reveals the flywheel effect: consistent momentum builds greatness. Level 5 Leadership (humble + fierce will), "First Who, Then What," and the Hedgehog Concept form the core. Greatness emerges from disciplined people, disciplined thought, and disciplined action.

Key Insight: Technology accelerators, not initiators. Relentless focus on what you can be best at.
